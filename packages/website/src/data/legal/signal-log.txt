Signal log, early access

A weekly record of the security signals of your public GitHub repositories, that you can check without us.

What you get today
Once a week, normally on Monday, we run the free check from tokenizen.nl/en/cra-check on the public repositories you name: up to 3 with Solo, up to 25 with Team. Each result becomes one line in a log file. Every line contains the hash of the line before it, so changing, removing or reordering an old line shows when you compare the log with a copy you received earlier or with a confirmed timestamp. The head of the log is timestamped with OpenTimestamps, which is outside our control, so the date can be checked without trusting us. A timestamp is pending at first and counts as confirmed only after Bitcoin has included it, usually some hours later. After each run we email you the updated log and the command to verify it. At this stage we run it ourselves, by hand.

What is not included yet
Email warnings when a signal that was visible is no longer visible. They are planned and are not part of what you buy today. There is also no public place where logs are published: your log is yours.

What this is not
The check looks at eight selected signals that are visible in a public repository, chosen with the EU Cyber Resilience Act in mind. It is not a compliance check, not a certificate and not legal advice. Not visible means not visible in the public repository and its GitHub releases; it may exist elsewhere. The limits are on the free check page and in our note about 109 repositories.
